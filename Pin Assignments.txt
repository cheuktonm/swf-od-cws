Arduino UNO R3 pin assignments

A0  - 				- Battery voltage measurement 
A1  - PIN_BTN_LEFT 	- Left button
A2  - PIN_BTN_RIGHT - Right button
A3  - PIN_BTN_OK 	- OK button
A4  * 				* conflicts with SDA
A5  * 				* conflicts with SCL

SCL - i2c bus clock > to LIDAR, MPU
SDA - i2c bus data  > to LIDAR, MPU

13  - PIN_TILT 		- Tilt sensor
12  - PIN_LCD_ES 	- LCD RS
11~ - PIN_LCD_EN 	- LCD Enable
10~ - PIN_SERVO 	~ Servo signal
9~  - PIN_US_TRIG 	- Ultrasonic Trigger
8   - PIN_US_ECHO 	- Ultrasonic Echo
7   - PIN_BZZ_LED 	- Alarm LED
6~  - PIN_BZZ_BZZ 	~ Alarm buzzer
5~  - PIN_LCD_D4 	- LCD D4
4   - PIN_LCD_D5 	- LCD D5
3~  - PIN_LCD_D6 	- LCD D6
2   - PIN_LCD_D7 	- LCD D7
1   *               * conflicts with UART tx
0   *               * conflicts with UART rx
